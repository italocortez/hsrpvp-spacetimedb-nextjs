### Scope clarifications (not deviations)

**1. Writable mapped-type extension at bracketAdvancement L480.** The plan's proposed `const updates: Partial<BracketMatch>` would fail because `.id.update()` requires the full row. **2. No fallback casts required at any mergeForUpdate call site.** Plan's fallback guidance (`mergeForUpdate(existing as HsrCharacter, incoming, HSR_CHARACTER_FIELDS)` per-site table) was not triggered because TypeScript collapses T to `any` cleanly when `existing` is `any`-typed, and `keyof any & string` resolves to `string`, making the `as const` arrays directly assignable to `readonly string[]`.
