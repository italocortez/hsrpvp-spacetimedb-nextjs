Reason: `mapToSynergyData` drops `draftMode`, so `Synergy[]` can't express the filter. Upstream filter achieves identical intent. Follow-up todo already logged at `.planning/todos/pending/2026-04-14-team-builder-synergy-auction-display.md`.

3. **`TableExplorer.tsx` admin delete PK payload** — still encodes pre-15.4 tuple for char/lc (runtime-wrong post-restructure, but TypeScript-green). Out of D-26 scope; Phase 17 owns the redesign. Plan-documented in `15.4-02-SUMMARY.md` Known Stubs.
