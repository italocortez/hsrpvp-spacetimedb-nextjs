## Other anonymous views scope (2nd round — deeper context)

**User's follow-up question:** *"what were these supposed to accomplish? i think lobby views was supposed to avoid having users subscribe to the lobby table entirely, that makes sense and should be for authed users as well, but wouldn't that be solved by the layered subscription strategy? what was view public account supposed to do?"*

**Response provided (from reading the view bodies + phase provenance):**

- `view_lobby_browser` (Phase 09 origin, Phase 15 D-01/D-02 relocation): anonymous BY DESIGN so visitors can browse active lobbies pre-auth. Privacy gate is the projection body — excludes timer settings, budget values, penalty settings, disconnect policy, audit columns, rosterVisibility, hostId; filters to active stages only (D-08). Applying Stage 2 gating would regress the pre-auth lobby discovery UX.
- `view_public_accounts` (Phase 10.4 D-20, D-22): replacement access path after `HsrAccount.public=false`. Filters to `isRosterPublic=true` accounts (user opt-in), rating only when `isRatingPublic=true` (separate opt-in), emits flat rows with optional character info. Anonymous BY DESIGN for pre-auth profile browsing.

**Key framing captured:** Both views use *server-side projection* as their privacy gate, not *subscription timing*. The Stage 2 gate solves a different problem (raw table, no projection, no auth check) and doesn't apply here. Applying it would regress intentional pre-auth UX.
