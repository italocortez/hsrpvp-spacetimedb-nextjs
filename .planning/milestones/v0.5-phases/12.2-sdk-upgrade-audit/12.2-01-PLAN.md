| T-12.2-03 | Information Disclosure | view_admin_user_private accessible in tablesSchema | mitigate | View is `public: true` in tablesSchema (required for subscription) but returns data only for admin-role callers — enforced server-side in the view body via `requireRole(ctx, 'Admin')`.
